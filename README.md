# E-Commerce Power BI Dashboard — Business Insights & Risk Detection

## Project Overview
This project analyzes an end-to-end *E-Commerce dataset* to uncover **hidden business risks, performance drivers, and decision-critical insights** using Power BI.

The dashboard is designed with a *CXO mindset*, focusing on:
- Revenue stability  
- Customer experience  
- Discount dependency  
- Behavioral patterns  

---

## Key Business Problems Addressed

### 1️⃣ Revenue Concentration Risk
- A large portion of revenue is driven by *high-value transactions*
- Business shows dependency on *few large orders/customers*
- Indicates **potential volatility and scalability risk**

---

### 2️⃣ Customer Experience vs Delivery Time
- Weak relationship between *delivery time* and *customer ratings*
- Suggests *product quality* impacts satisfaction more than logistics
- Indicates need to improve **product experience, not just delivery**

---

### 3️⃣ Discount Dependency
- ~48% of total discount concentrated in *Electronics category*
- Revenue contribution is similar across categories, but discounting is not
- Indicates **margin leakage due to aggressive discounting**

---

### 4️⃣ Category Revenue Imbalance
- ~80% of revenue comes from:
  - Electronics  
  - Sports  
  - Home & Garden  
- Shows **over-dependence on limited categories**

## ⚠️ Key Insights
-  Revenue is *highly concentrated* → potential business risk  
-  Discounts are driving *artificial growth* in some categories  
-  Customer satisfaction is *not strongly delivery-driven*  
-  Business depends heavily on *top-performing categories*  

---

## Business Recommendations
- Reduce dependency on high-value transactions  
- Optimize discount strategies to protect margins  
- Improve product quality to boost ratings  
- Diversify category performance to stabilize revenue
